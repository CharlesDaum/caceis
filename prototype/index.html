How Absence Risk Is Calculated

1. Target

An employee is labeled positive when recorded absence days are at least .

2. Features

The model uses tenure, training intensity, review completion, absorptive capacity, and country/contract/gender/age/entry-reason segments.

3. Model

A class-balanced logistic regression estimates the probability that the employee belongs to the high-absence group.

4. Bands

The raw probability is then mapped to Low, Medium, or High risk so HR teams can prioritize action more easily.
